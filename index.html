Our Story

A Table That Feels Like Home

Est. Jhenaidah

Le Alimento — "The Food", Made with Love

In the historic district of Jhenaidah, Le Alimento began as a simple dream: to give every family a beautiful place to gather, celebrate, and simply enjoy exceptional food — from our slow-cooked kacchi biryani to flame-grilled kebabs and indulgent desserts.

Named from the heart of Italian cuisine — alimento means "food" — our kitchen blends time-honoured Bangladeshi recipes with a touch of global elegance, served in an ambience of warm light, refined décor, and unhurried comfort.
